New: usage history and run-out forecast.

- Every refresh is logged on-device (3 weeks kept, deleted on logout).
- The app charts session (24 h) and weekly/per-model (3 weeks) usage, with
  the current trend extended as a dashed line once there is enough data.
- The widget and notification warn when the trend reaches 100% before the
  window resets.
- Tapping the widget or notification now opens the app; the notification's
  Refresh button and the widget's refresh icon update in place.
