Correctness and robustness fixes from a full code review:

- Overlapping background refreshes no longer race the single-use refresh
  token, which could force a spurious re-login.
- A transient token-endpoint error (rate limit, proxy page) no longer marks
  the login permanently broken; only a real invalid_grant does.
- A network failure mid-exchange no longer replays a single-use code or
  refresh token against the second endpoint.
- OAuth tokens are excluded from Android backup and device transfer.
- The notification permission is asked once, and only re-asked by a real
  toggle, instead of on every app start.
- The keyboard no longer covers the authorization-code field.
- The settings screen updates itself when a background refresh or a login
  finishes, including after a rotation.
- Notification percent colors now follow light/dark switches immediately.
